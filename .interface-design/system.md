# BD2US Interface System

## Product character

BD2US is a calm admissions field guide for Bangladeshi students working through a high-stakes, unfamiliar process. It should feel editorial, grounded, and exact rather than like a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Typography

- Body and controls: self-hosted Atkinson Hyperlegible.
- Editorial headings: self-hosted Literata Variable.
- Long-form reading measure: 68-72 characters.
- Four text levels: display, section heading, body, metadata.

## Color

- Forest green: primary navigation and action.
- Warm paper: base reading surface.
- Passport navy: structure, records, and institutional context.
- Deadline amber: dates and time-sensitive attention.
- Visa-stamp red: genuine warnings only.
- Status must always include text or iconography; never color alone.

## Depth and shape

- Use quiet surface shifts and low-opacity borders as the only depth strategy.
- Avoid decorative gradients, arbitrary accent colors, and shadow-heavy generic cards.
- Use 4px spacing increments and three practical radii: 0.55rem, 0.9rem, 1.35rem.
- Prefer purpose-built records, checklists, deadline strips, and comparison rows over repeated cards.

## Signature pattern

The journey rail appears across the field guide, college research, roadmap, dashboard, and glossary. It answers: where am I, what is complete, what matters in the Fall 2027 cycle, and what should I do next?

## Interaction and accessibility

- Minimum 44px touch targets for primary controls.
- Visible focus with a high-contrast amber outline.
- Semantic headings, lists, tables, and status labels.
- Respect reduced motion and retain all meaning in print and offline states.
- Personalization changes emphasis but never hides public guidance.
- Text inputs use an inset surface-color shift for keyboard focus, without an outline or inset underline; buttons and links retain the visible amber focus outline.

## Research controls

- Large filter and search workspaces remain in normal document flow. They do not stick over records or use translucent floating surfaces.
- Ranking systems remain visibly separate by institution category and edition. Unranked records are labeled, then ordered alphabetically.

## Data trust

- Show dataset and review dates near cycle-sensitive facts.
- Distinguish baseline dataset information, official verification, and community perspective.
- Missing facts remain explicitly unavailable.
- Acceptance rate is institutional context, never a personal probability.
